Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1 · Dec 15

Arrival in Rovaniemi & Glass Igloo Check-in

Arrive at Rovaniemi airport where our driver will be waiting. Transfer directly to the Arctic SnowHotel & Glass Igloos. Settle into your 360-degree glass igloo and enjoy a welcome dinner featuring traditional Lappish cuisine.

Arctic SnowHotel & Glass Igloos Dinner included
Day 2 · Dec 16

Husky Safari & Northern Lights Hunt

After breakfast, head to a local husky farm. Learn how to mush your own team of enthusiastic huskies through the snow-covered forests. In the evening, join an expert guide for a Northern Lights hunting expedition by snowmobile away from city lights.

6 Hours total activities Breakfast & Campfire snacks included
Day 3 · Dec 17

Reindeer Farm Visit & Santa Claus Village

Meet traditional reindeer herders and enjoy a peaceful sleigh ride. Afterwards, cross the magical Arctic Circle at Santa Claus Village. Meet Santa, send postcards from his official post office, and enjoy free time for shopping.

Day 4 · Dec 18

Departure

Enjoy a final Nordic breakfast before your private transfer takes you back to Rovaniemi Airport for your onward journey.
