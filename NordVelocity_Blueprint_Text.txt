1. Executive Summary
NordVelocity is to be built as a premium, secure, scalable digital platform combining two clearly separated customerjourneys: (1) private chauffeur/transportation and (2) curated tours, events and experiences. The platform must beowner-controlled, mobile-first, SEO-ready, maintainable by future development teams, and designed to expand fromFinland into the wider Nordic region.
This is not a theme-customization project. It is a business platform. The developer must design the architecture, datamodel, admin tools, security controls, booking workflows and handover process so that NordVelocity can operate withoutpermanent dependence on the original developer.
2. Non-Negotiable Ownership and Control
All critical assets must be created under accounts controlled by NordVelocity. The developer receives only the accessneeded to perform the work.
NordVelocity must own and control:
\177 Domain and DNS
\177 Hosting/cloud account
\177 Source-code repository and complete Git history
\177 Production and staging environments
\177 Databases and backups
\177 Payment accounts and payout configuration
\177 Mapping/API accounts and keys
\177 Transactional email/SMS accounts
\177 Analytics, tag manager and search-console properties
\177 CDN/WAF/security accounts
\177 All custom source code, design files and documentation
\177 Licenses purchased specifically for NordVelocity, where license terms allow transfer/ownership
No critical service may depend permanently on a developer\222s personal account. No hidden backdoors, undocumentedadministrator accounts, encrypted/obfuscated business logic, or vendor lock-in are acceptable. Developer access mustbe removable after handover.
3. Project Architecture
The system must be modular. Taxi/chauffeur, tours/experiences, bookings, payments, customers, content, SEO,notifications and reporting should be separate logical modules connected through documented interfaces.
The architecture must support future replacement or extension of individual modules without rebuilding the entireplatform. Business rules must be configurable from the administration area wherever reasonably possible. Prices,vehicles, routes, tour dates, capacities, content and SEO must never require source-code edits for normal operations.

5. World-Class Design Direction
NordVelocity must feel like a premium Scandinavian travel and mobility brand\227not a conventional taxi website.
Design principles:
\177 Scandinavian minimalism: generous space, strong typography, restrained decoration
\177 Premium, calm and trustworthy rather than flashy
\177 Near-black, white/off-white and one controlled accent colour
\177 Avoid excessive gold, gradients, glow effects, stock \223luxury\224 clich\351s and unnecessary animation
\177 High-quality editorial imagery with consistent ratios
\177 Clear visual hierarchy and short decision paths
\177 WCAG-aware contrast, focus states, keyboard navigation and accessible forms
\177 Responsive design created mobile-first, not desktop-first
The developer/designer must provide a small design system containing typography, spacing scale, colours, buttons, formfields, cards, navigation, alerts, icons, imagery rules and responsive behaviour.
Homepage hero: premium Nordic/Finnish travel landscape blended naturally with a black metallic luxury vehicle, with notaxi roof sign. The first decision must be two clear CTAs: \223Explore Tours\224 and \223Book a Chauffeur.\224 The headline mustrepresent year-round experiences, not only winter/Arctic tourism.

6. Information Architecture and Separate Customer Journeys
The platform must preserve one NordVelocity brand while keeping the two commercial journeys distinct.
Tour journey:
Home \256 Tours \256 Destination/Experience Search \256 Tour Detail \256 Date/Guests \256 Checkout \256 Confirmation
Chauffeur journey:
Home \256 Chauffeur \256 Pickup/Destination/Service \256 Quote \256 Vehicle \256 Checkout \256 Confirmation
Tour pages may contain a subtle secondary chauffeur cross-sell. Chauffeur pages may contain subtle tourrecommendations lower in the page or after booking. Cross-selling must never interrupt or confuse the primary bookingflow.

\177 Hourly chauffeur service

\177 Passenger and luggage capacity rules
\177 Availability controls
\177 Date/time-based rules
\177 Location/zone-based rules
\177 Optional surcharges/discount rules

Vehicle management must include name, class, photos, passenger capacity, luggage capacity, features, active/inactivestate and pricing configuration.
The design must leave room for future driver assignment, corporate accounts, minivans, buses, live tracking andpartner/API integrations without requiring a complete rebuild.

17. Security Architecture
Security is a design requirement.
Minimum controls:
\177 HTTPS everywhere
\177 Secure headers and modern TLS configuration
\177 Multi-factor authentication for privileged accounts where supported
\177 Role-based access and least privilege
\177 Strong password policy
\177 Protection against common web vulnerabilities (OWASP Top 10)
\177 Server-side validation and authorization
\177 CSRF protection
\177 Secure session/cookie settings
\177 Rate limiting for sensitive endpoints
\177 Bot/spam protection appropriate to forms and authentication
\177 Secrets stored outside source code
\177 Restricted API keys
\177 Encryption in transit and appropriate encryption at rest
\177 Dependency/security update process
\177 Centralized error/security logging
\177 Audit logs for critical admin actions
\177 WAF/CDN protection where appropriate
\177 Regular vulnerability review
\177 No production debug information exposed to customers
A pre-launch security review is required. High/critical findings must be resolved before launch.
